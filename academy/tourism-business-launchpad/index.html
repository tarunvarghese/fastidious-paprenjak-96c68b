THE COURSE

Build your tourism business with clarity.

Tourism Business Launchpad is designed for people who want to enter the tourism industry with a clear understanding of what it takes to build and launch a real tourism business.

WHAT YOU WILL LEARN

From idea to launch.

The course takes you through the essential foundations required to understand, structure and prepare a tourism business for the real world.

01

Understand Tourism

Understand how the tourism industry works, where opportunities exist and how different tourism businesses create value.

02

Find Your Opportunity

Identify your market, niche, ideal customer and the opportunity your business can realistically serve.

03

Build Your Business Foundation

Develop the foundations of your tourism business, from your business model and product to positioning and operational thinking.

04

Prepare to Go to Market

Build the essential marketing, digital presence and customer acquisition foundations needed before launch.

05

Launch With a Roadmap

Bring everything together into a practical launch roadmap that helps you move from planning to action.

WHO THIS IS FOR

01

Aspiring Entrepreneurs

02

New Tourism Businesses

For early-stage founders who need structure, direction and a practical roadmap.

03

Tourism Professionals

For professionals who want to understand the business side of tourism more deeply.
