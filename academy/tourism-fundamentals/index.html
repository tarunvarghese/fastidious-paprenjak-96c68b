THE FOUNDATION

Before you build better tourism, understand it.

Tourism Fundamentals is the starting point of the Vargiz learning journey. It introduces the essential concepts, structures and ideas needed to understand the tourism industry before moving into more specialised learning.

WHAT YOU WILL LEARN

Understand the world of tourism.

Build a strong foundation before moving into tourism business, marketing, operations and professional development.

01

Understanding Tourism

Understand what tourism is, how it works and why it matters across destinations, communities and economies.

02

The Tourism Ecosystem

Explore the people, businesses, destinations, organisations and systems that come together to create tourism.

03

Tourism Products & Experiences

Understand how destinations, products, services and experiences come together to create value for travellers.

04

The Traveller Journey

Develop a foundation for understanding travellers, their expectations and the journey from inspiration to memory.

05

Tourism Businesses

Understand the different types of tourism businesses and how they contribute to the wider industry.

06

The Foundations of Professional Tourism

Develop the fundamental perspective needed to think, communicate and operate professionally within tourism.

WHO SHOULD START HERE

Built for people ready to understand tourism properly.

01

Beginners

Anyone who wants a clear introduction to the tourism industry and how it works.

02

Aspiring Professionals

People preparing to build a career in tourism, travel and hospitality.

03

Future Entrepreneurs

People planning to start a tourism business and looking for the right foundation.

04

Existing Professionals

Tourism professionals who want to revisit the fundamentals and strengthen their foundation.
